# Release log

One log for the whole developer account: every store submission across your apps, what went in,
what came back, and what you learned. Read it before every submission. Add a row the day something
is submitted, and update it the day the store answers.

Keep it somewhere private. It will hold details about your account.

## Lessons

Each lesson names the event that taught it. Newest first. Start from the ones in LESSONS.md and
add your own.
